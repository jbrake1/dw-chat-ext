How we help you get found, and stay in touch

You know your customers by name. Your website should make it just as easy for the rest of the island — and the people coming to see it — to find you.

Your website, on your domain

Hours, location, photos, and how to reach you, on a site that works on a phone. We host it, switch on HTTPS, and point your own domain to it. You can also have an AI assistant design the pages and upload them for you.

Found by neighbors and visitors

Locals look for who they already trust. Visitors often search before they land. We help you show up clearly for both, with a site that is easy to find and easy to use.

Your customers, in one place

Keep the people and companies you work with, plus notes and photos from the job. When you are ready, email them from that same list.

A store on your own site new

Sell products from your domain: a catalog, cart, checkout, invoices, and shipping with tracking. Online card checkout is not connected yet, so you record each payment yourself. See how the store works.

Email for your regulars coming soon

Send hours, seasonal specials, or a simple note to the customers already in your records. The list is yours today; newsletters are on the way.

Sign in with your email

No password to remember. We email you a sign-in link or a short code, and you stay in control of your site.

Sell online with the DW Alliance store new

Add a store to your own website. Customers browse your products, fill a cart, and check out on your domain, and you see every order, payment, and shipment in one place.

Products and categories

Prices, sizes or options, stock counts, photos, and descriptions, grouped into categories customers can browse and search.

Cart and checkout

Customers sign in with an emailed code, no password, pick a shipping and billing address, and place the order. Each order becomes an invoice.

Payments and shipping

Record payments against each invoice and ship with carrier and tracking links. Online card checkout is not connected yet, so payments are confirmed by you and no one is charged automatically.
